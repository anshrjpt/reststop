The people who deliver our dinner deserve a place to rest.

Delivery riders spend long hours on the road with nowhere to use a toilet, drink water or charge their phone. RestStop maps those places — built by riders, for riders.

How it works

  1. Find

    Riders see the nearest toilet, water, charging point or shade — filtered for what they need right now.

  2. Verify

    Every visit is a chance to confirm a place still works, so the map stays trustworthy.

  3. Welcome

    Shops sign up as rider-friendly and open their doors — a small act that changes a rider's day.
